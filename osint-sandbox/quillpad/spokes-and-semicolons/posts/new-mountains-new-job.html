New mountains, new job

After six and a half years writing logistics software back in Minnesota, I have swapped Lake Superior for the Rockies. Last week I started as a backend developer at a small startup that builds trail maps, which means I now get paid to think about bike routes. My younger self would be thrilled.

The move was two days of driving with a border collie who refused to sleep. The new place is small, but I can see mountains from the kitchen window and there is a bike path at the end of the street.

Still getting used to the altitude. Walking up the stairs with groceries is a workout.
