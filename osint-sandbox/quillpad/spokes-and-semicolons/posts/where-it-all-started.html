Where it all started: room 14 at Birchmoor

People ask how I got into programming. The honest answer is room 14 at Birchmoor Elementary, my first school, back home in Duluth. Room 14 had six beige computers that nobody else wanted to use at lunch, and Mrs. Halvorsen let me stay in with them if I promised not to break anything. I broke several things.

My dad says I get it from my grandpa Teodor, who I am named after. He died years before I was born, but apparently he also took apart everything he owned just to see how it worked. Family legend says he once rebuilt a radio from two broken ones.

If you are a teacher who lets a weird kid use the computers at lunch: thank you. It matters.
