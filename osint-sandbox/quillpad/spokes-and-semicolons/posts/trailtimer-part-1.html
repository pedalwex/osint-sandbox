Building TrailTimer, part 1

I have been timing my rides with a mess of spreadsheets for two years, so I finally wrote a proper tool. TrailTimer is a small Flask app: you log a ride, it stores the start and end times, and it draws a chart of your times on each segment.

The code is public on my GitHub: github.com/pedalwex/trailtimer. It is rough, the SQL is embarrassing, and pull requests are welcome.

Two lessons so far. First, store every timestamp in UTC and only convert to local time when you display it. My live site showed every ride six hours in the future until someone on CodeCircle pointed that out. Second, I made a rookie mistake with a config file along the way. I fixed it, and I will write about it in part 2 once I stop being embarrassed.
