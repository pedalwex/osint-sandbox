Meet Pixel

Three days ago I drove to the animal shelter "just to look". I came home with Pixel, a black-and-white border collie who is about a year old and has more energy than my laptop battery.

In her first 72 hours she has learned to open the pantry, herded the cat next door, and figured out that the sound of my bike shoes means we are going outside. Duluth winters are going to be interesting.

My childhood pets were a hamster and some fish, so this is a big step up. Wish me luck.
