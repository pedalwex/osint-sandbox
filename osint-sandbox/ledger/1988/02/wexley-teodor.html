Obituaries · 12 February 1988

Teodor Wexley, 71

Teodor Wexley, 71, of Duluth, died peacefully at home on 9 February 1988. He was born in 1916 in Hibbing and worked for thirty-one years as a machinist at the ore docks, where colleagues knew him as the man who could fix anything with a screwdriver and a length of wire.

Teodor loved fishing on Lake Superior, polka on Saturday nights, and taking apart radios, clocks and anything else that stood still long enough. He was preceded in death by his wife, Helena.

He is survived by his son Andrei (Marta, née Kovač) of Duluth, his sister Irena of Hibbing, and many nieces and nephews.

Visitation will be held Monday at the Lakeside Chapel. In lieu of flowers, the family asks for donations to the Duluth Public Library.
